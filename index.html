ABOUT ME

I enjoy exploring different cultures, discovering new places, hiking in the mountains, and appreciating art, history, and heritage. I have a particular interest in Filipino culture, Baybayin calligraphy, and traditional arts. I also love baking, especially Asian pastries and Filipino desserts, and enjoy experimenting with new recipes. Music is another important part of my life, particularly playing the piano. As a self-taught pianist, I am continuously learning and refining my skills. For many years, I have specialized in Black gospel, Southern gospel, and jazz, and more recently, I have expanded into classical music. Two pieces I particularly love are Rachmaninoff’s Rhapsody on a Theme of Paganini and Debussy’s Clair de Lune. I also appreciate quiet scenic getaways, beautiful architecture, and meaningful time with family. I am naturally curious and enjoy lifelong learning, creative pursuits, and finding inspiration in the beauty of everyday life.
